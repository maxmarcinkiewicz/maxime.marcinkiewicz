About

Ten years in IT, five of them in Power Apps

I started in IT ten years ago and have spent the last five years as a Microsoft Power Apps developer. I work with teams that need a tool fitted to the way they actually work: an approval process, an inventory tracker, a field-service app, a customer portal.

As a consultant I have delivered for clients in pharmaceuticals, banking, logistics, automotive and energy, in the United States, Germany, Austria, Italy, Bulgaria, France and Poland. I also do the business analysis, so the app solves the right problem. I take a project from the first conversation to a working app in your Microsoft 365 tenant: data model in Dataverse or SharePoint, the app itself, automations in Power Automate, and handover so your team can run it. Today I also integrate Claude into these solutions, so apps can read documents, draft replies and summarise records.
